Data Deletion

How to Delete Your Account and Data

You can permanently delete your Bloom account and all associated data directly from the app:

  1. Open the Bloom app
  2. Go to Settings (gear icon in the top-right corner)
  3. Scroll to Account actions
  4. Tap Delete account
  5. Confirm the deletion in the dialog

What Gets Deleted

  • Your user profile (name, email)
  • All saved products and their expiry dates
  • All notification settings and scheduled reminders
  • Your authentication credentials

What Remains

  • Anonymous contributions to the shared barcode cache (product name, brand, nutrition data associated with a barcode — these are not linked to your identity)

Timeframe

Account deletion is immediate and irreversible. All data is removed as soon as you confirm the deletion.

Alternative: Contact Us

If you cannot access the app, you can request account deletion by emailing support@bloomapp.io from the email address associated with your account. We will process your request within 7 business days.
