Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 6, 2026

Bloom ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Bloom mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo (if available). This is used solely to identify your account.

Product Data

You provide product information including product names, brands, barcodes, expiry dates, storage locations, quantities, and nutritional information. This data is stored to provide the core functionality of the app.

Camera Data

The app uses your device camera to scan barcodes and read text (OCR) from product labels. Images are processed entirely on your device using on-device machine learning and are never uploaded, stored, or transmitted to any server.

Usage Data

We collect anonymous crash reports and basic analytics (app opens, feature usage) through Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to improve app stability and performance.

Device Tokens

We store device tokens to deliver push notification reminders about expiring products. These tokens are not shared with third parties.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the app's core features (tracking expiry dates, sending reminders)
  • To identify you and sync your data across devices
  • To improve app performance and fix bugs
  • To send you push notification reminders you've configured

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) with encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest. Access to your product data is restricted to your authenticated account only through Firestore Security Rules.

4. Barcode Cache (Shared Data)

When you save a product with a barcode, the product name, brand, category, and nutritional information (but NOT your personal data, expiry dates, or account information) may be contributed to a shared barcode lookup cache. This helps other users get instant product information when scanning the same barcode. This shared data is anonymous and cannot be traced back to your account.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We share data only with:

  • Firebase (Google) — for authentication, data storage, analytics, and crash reporting
  • Open Food Facts — we query their public API using barcodes to fetch product information (no personal data is sent)

6. Your Rights

  • Access: You can export all your data from Settings > Export my data
  • Deletion: You can permanently delete your account and all associated data from Settings > Delete account
  • Portability: Data export is available in JSON format

7. Children's Privacy

Bloom is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

8. Third-Party Services

  • Firebase Authentication (Google)
  • Cloud Firestore (Google)
  • Firebase Analytics (Google)
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google)
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google)
  • Open Food Facts API

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
